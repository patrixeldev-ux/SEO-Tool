Questions without answers

What is the best grind size for French press?

French press brewing is the opposite approach: full immersion and a metal mesh that lets the natural oils through. The result is a heavier, rounder cup with a lot of texture. Use a coarse grind, steep for four minutes, and press slowly. Pour it all out promptly, because coffee left sitting on the grounds keeps extracting and quickly turns harsh. The island of beans we call a blend is really a recipe. Each component brings something: sweetness from one lot, acidity from another, body from a third. We cup them separately, then in pairs, then together, and only keep the combination that tastes better than any single ingredient would on its own.

Why does my coffee taste bitter?

  • Grind too fine
  • Water too hot
  • Brew too long

More background

A burr grinder is the single most useful upgrade for home brewing. Blades chop beans into uneven pieces, so some particles over extract and turn bitter while others under extract and taste sour. Burrs crush the beans between two abrasive surfaces and produce a far more consistent size, which means the water can do its work evenly and the cup tastes balanced.
