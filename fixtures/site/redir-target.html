Where you landed

We roast on a small electric machine that recirculates its own hot air, which cuts our energy use and keeps the smoke down in a street full of houses. Neighbours sometimes stop by just to say how pleasant the aroma is, and we take that as a better review than any online rating.

If you are new to specialty coffee, begin with a medium roast from a familiar origin such as Colombia or Brazil. These coffees are approachable, with nutty and chocolate flavours that most people enjoy. Once you are comfortable, explore a washed Ethiopian or Kenyan lot to discover just how fruity and tea-like a cup of coffee can be.

Our wholesale programme supplies restaurants, offices and small hotels across the region. We install the equipment, train the staff and visit regularly to check that the grinders are clean and the recipes are dialled in. A great espresso depends as much on maintenance as on beans, and consistent service keeps our partners happy.

Nothing wrong here

Water makes up almost all of every cup, so it deserves attention. Soft, clean water with a moderate mineral balance pulls out sweetness and clarity, while very hard water can mute the flavour and leave scale inside your kettle. If your tap water tastes flat, try a simple carbon filter before spending money on expensive equipment or more expensive beans.

French press brewing is the opposite approach: full immersion and a metal mesh that lets the natural oils through. The result is a heavier, rounder cup with a lot of texture. Use a coarse grind, steep for four minutes, and press slowly. Pour it all out promptly, because coffee left sitting on the grounds keeps extracting and quickly turns harsh.
