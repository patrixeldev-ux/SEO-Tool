Cold brew questions

Cold brew is forgiving and easy to make at home. Combine coarsely ground coffee with cold water, stir gently, and leave the jar in the refrigerator overnight. Strain it through a fine filter in the morning and you have a smooth, low acid concentrate that keeps for about a week and can be diluted with water, milk or ice to taste.

How long does cold brew last?

Properly strained cold brew concentrate keeps for about seven days in a sealed jar in the refrigerator. Flavour is best in the first four days.

Can I heat cold brew?

Yes. Dilute the concentrate with hot water; it tastes smoother and less bitter than regular drip coffee.

Cold brew ratios

Grind size is the quickest lever you have when a brew tastes wrong. If the cup is sour, thin or salty, the water has not extracted enough, so grind finer or brew a little longer. If it is bitter, harsh or hollow, the water has extracted too much, so grind coarser or shorten the brew. Make one change at a time.

Storage is straightforward. Keep your beans in an airtight container away from sunlight, heat and moisture, and grind only what you need for each brew. The refrigerator is a poor choice because it introduces condensation and absorbs odours from other food. A cool cupboard is perfect, and a one way valve bag keeps oxygen out between uses.

Freshness matters more than most people expect. Coffee is at its best between one and four weeks after roasting, once the trapped gas has settled but before the aromatic oils fade. That is why every bag we sell carries the roast date rather than a vague best before stamp, and why we roast in small batches several times a week instead of stockpiling inventory.
