Water makes up almost all of every cup, so it deserves attention. Soft, clean water with a moderate mineral balance pulls out sweetness and clarity, while very hard water can mute the flavour and leave scale inside your kettle. If your tap water tastes flat, try a simple carbon filter before spending money on expensive equipment or more expensive beans.

French press brewing is the opposite approach: full immersion and a metal mesh that lets the natural oils through. The result is a heavier, rounder cup with a lot of texture. Use a coarse grind, steep for four minutes, and press slowly. Pour it all out promptly, because coffee left sitting on the grounds keeps extracting and quickly turns harsh.

Packaging is a constant puzzle. Our bags are made from a single recyclable material so that they can be collected curbside, and we ship in cartons made of recycled fibre with paper tape. Subscriptions are the easiest way to keep fresh beans arriving without thinking about it. Choose a size, a roast and a schedule, and we will roast on the morning of dispatch. You can pause, skip or change the order at any time through a simple email link, and there is never a minimum commitment or a cancellation fee.

Grind size is the quickest lever you have when a brew tastes wrong. If the cup is sour, thin or salty, the water has not extracted enough, so grind finer or brew a little longer. If it is bitter, harsh or hollow, the water has extracted too much, so grind coarser or shorten the brew. Make one change at a time.

Harbor & Pine began in a rented boathouse where the first roaster, a secondhand drum machine, shared floor space with coils of rope and a bench of old tools. Every morning the smell of toasting beans drifted across the water and drew fishermen in from the dock. They asked for a cup, then a bag, and within a year the little shop had a line out of the door before sunrise.
